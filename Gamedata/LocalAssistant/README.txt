LocalAssistant v1.0
--------------
LocalAssistant is an assistance mod specifically made for KSP to help you in-game. It:
- runs a specific list of local language models (AI that can run locally on your machine without internet and limits) with KSP info.
- contains every single data about your KSP directory, but CANNOT modify them, so you can ask it anything if there are issues/needed stuff.
===========================================================================================================================================
LLMs:

- Qwen 2.5 3B
- Gemma 4 E4B
- Qwen 3.5 9B
- Gemma 4 12B QAT
- Gemma 4 31B QAT

Each one needs to be downloaded to load and chat with. You may download any of them via the in-game GUI.
===========================================================================================================================================
GUI:

- You can access previously saved conversations with your local language model.
- You can select any of your downloaded LLMs to chat with.
- You can rename and delete previously saved conversations.
- You can resize the GUI through the bottom right corner.
